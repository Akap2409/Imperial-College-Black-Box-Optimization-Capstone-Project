# References

## Optimisation

Srinivas, N., Krause, A., Kakade, S. M., and Seeger, M. (2010). *Gaussian Process Optimization in the Bandit Setting: No Regret and Experimental Design.* Proceedings of the 27th International Conference on Machine Learning. [Paper](https://www.dna.caltech.edu/Papers/Srinivas-2010-regret-ICML.pdf)

This work motivates the Upper Confidence Bound (UCB) acquisition concept used in the retrospective workflow.

Mitchell, M., Wu, S., Zaldivar, A., Barnes, P., Vasserman, L., Hutchinson, B., Spitzer, E., Raji, I. D., and Gebru, T. (2019). *Model Cards for Model Reporting.* FAT* '19, 220-229. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3287560.3287596)

The datasheet and model card in this repository follow the transparency principles introduced by these papers.
